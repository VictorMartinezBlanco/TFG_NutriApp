\chapter*{Abstract}

\section*{\tituloPortadaEngVal}

This Bachelor's Thesis presents the design of NutriApp, a web platform aimed at centralizing the workflow of professional nutritionists. The idea emerged from observing first-hand how a nutritionist manages his daily practice: his information was scattered across physical notebooks, Google Forms, Google Calendar, his clinic's own platform, phone notes, and WhatsApp. Getting the full picture of a single client meant manually cross-referencing data from all of these tools, which was inefficient and made it easy for important details to slip through the cracks.

Based on this real problem, the goal was to design a web application that brings all of these functionalities together into a single point of access. The platform is built around two separate panels: one for the nutritionist, where they can manage clients, appointments, forms, nutritional plans, and private notes from one place; and one for the client, providing a dedicated interface to view their plan, log daily habits, communicate with their nutritionist, and track their progress.

Beyond replacing the scattered tools, NutriApp introduces features that are only possible when all data is connected: a smart alert system that detects risk patterns from client data, gamified daily check-ins with streaks to encourage plan adherence, shopping lists with substitutions based on nutritional equivalence, a customizable form builder with associated alert rules, an integrated food database with barcode scanning and AI-based photo recognition, a marketplace of educational resources that gives the nutritionist an additional monetization channel, and an automations engine that allows them to model recurring procedures of their clinical practice. Communication with the client happens through an integrated chat with video and voice calls, removing the dependency on external tools. The database design, developed in collaboration with the thesis supervisors, reflects the complexity of these features and constitutes the core of the project.
